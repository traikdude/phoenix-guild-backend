# The guild ledger

**Roles and audience.** A title illustration for seekers and maintainers visiting phoenix-guild-backend, also sized for the GitHub social preview. The ruled form in Index.html and the Submissions sheet in Code.gs supply the two facing pages.

**Primary prompt.** Wide 2:1 panoramic editorial woodcut illustration of an open guild assessment ledger, ruled cream paper on a dark oxblood desk, a large vermilion and copper phoenix printed across the left hand leaf, its long ornate feathers becoming crisp ruled columns on the right leaf. Dramatic sophisticated Arts and Crafts book illustration, limited spot inks, beautiful generous negative space, flat overhead composition, high contrast. No words, letters, numbers, logos, or writing whatsoever.

**Alternate prompt.** Design a two-page guild register in flat bookplate style. Stamp a stylized phoenix in brick-red ink on the left. Rule the opposite leaf into empty rows and columns. Use parchment, aged brass and oxblood, with a strong silhouette at thumbnail size. No lettering or interface elements.

**Text in art.** None. The README supplies the project title.

**Alt text.** A copper-red phoenix spreads its wings across an open book beside a ruled submissions ledger.

**Placement.** Use banner.png at the top of README.md and social-preview.png for manual GitHub upload. Both are 1280 × 640. The independently drawn banner.svg uses opaque paper and ink colors for light and dark backgrounds.

**Refresh when** the assessment’s ruled-paper design, guild identity or spreadsheet destination changes. Keep real answers and identifiers out of the artwork.
